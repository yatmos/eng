### **Appointment at KIST to Advance Climate Research**

Prof. Emeritus Seongsoo Yum, a distinguished scholar in cloud physics, has been appointed principal research scientist at the Climate and Environment Research Institute of the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST). He will draw on his expertise in cloud physics and use cloud chamber experiments to advance research on technologies addressing the climate crisis.
