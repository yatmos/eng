####  Junwoo Lee and Jieun Shin – Excellence Awards at the 2026 OASIS Conference
At the 2026 Ocean and Atmosphere Science Interdisciplinary Studies (OASIS) Conference, researcher Junwoo Lee received the Excellence Award from the Director General of the Korea Hydrographic and Oceanographic Agency, and researcher Jieun Shin received the Excellence Award from the OASIS Steering Committee Chair.
