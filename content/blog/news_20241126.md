####  Jieun Shin and Seungok Oh – Grand Prize at the 4th Ocean Science Big Data Competition
Undergraduate students Jieun Shin and Seungok Oh received the Grand Prize (Minister of Oceans and Fisheries Award) at the 4th Ocean Science Big Data Competition, supported by the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries, co-hosted by the Korea Institute of Marine Science & Technology Promotion (KIMST) and The Korean Society of Oceanography, and organized by Haebom Data Co., Ltd.
