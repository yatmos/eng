### **Humboldt Research Award**

Prof. Jhoon Kim has received the Humboldt Research Award from the Alexander von Humboldt Foundation in Germany. The award recognizes his contributions to developing and applying satellite remote sensing techniques to observe atmospheric constituents, including air pollutants and greenhouse gases.
