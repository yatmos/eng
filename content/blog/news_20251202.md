####  Gyorim Song, Hasom So, and Chaeyeon Lee – Grand Prize at the 5th Ocean Science Big Data Competition
Undergraduate students Gyorim Song, Hasom So, and Chaeyeon Lee received the Grand Prize (Minister of Oceans and Fisheries Award) at the 5th Ocean Science Big Data Competition, supported by the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries, co-hosted by the Korea Institute of Marine Science & Technology Promotion (KIMST) and The Korean Society of Oceanography, and organized by MarineAct Co., Ltd.
