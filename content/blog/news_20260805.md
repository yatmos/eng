### **Research Support for Distinguished Scholars**

Prof. Jhoon Kim has been selected for a Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT) program supporting distinguished scholars in Korea. Prof. Jhoon Kim will use AI and geostationary satellite data to study the three-dimensional distribution of air pollutants over Asia and the processes that form tropospheric ozone.
