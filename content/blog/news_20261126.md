#### Dr. Junghee Yun Selected for a Grade 5 Ocean Meteorology and Climate Position at the Korea Meteorological Administration

Dr. Junghee Yun, a postdoctoral researcher in the Air-Sea Modeling Laboratory (advisor: Prof. Hajoon Song), was selected for a Grade 5 position in ocean meteorology and climate at the Korea Meteorological Administration through the 2026 national civil service recruitment examination for experienced professionals at Grades 5 and 7. Congratulations!
