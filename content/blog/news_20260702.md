#### Gyorim Song, Jieun Shin, Seungju Oh, and Chaeyeon Lee Win the Top Prize at the AX Ideas Competition

A team of undergraduate and graduate students from our department—Gyorim Song, Jieun Shin, Seungju Oh, and Chaeyeon Lee—won the Top Prize in the Assigned Analysis category of the 2026 AX (AI Transformation) Ideas Competition. The Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment hosted the competition with the participation of 24 affiliated public institutions, including K-water.
