#### Undergraduate Club Gisang Cheonoe Wins Gold at the 2026 KMS Fall Meeting

Gisang Cheonoe, an undergraduate academic club in Yonsei University’s Department of Atmospheric Sciences, won the Gold Award after presenting its work at the 2026 Fall Meeting of the Korean Meteorological Society. The club was part of the second cohort of the society’s support program for undergraduate meteorology and climate clubs.
