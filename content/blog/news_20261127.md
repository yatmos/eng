#### Undergraduate Songyeon Kim Appointed as a Grade 5 Meteorological Officer

Songyeon Kim, an undergraduate who entered Yonsei University in 2022, passed the national Grade 5 open competitive civil service examination and was appointed as a Grade 5 meteorological officer. Congratulations!
